Questions

Before your first visit

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes, at both offices. Book online or call us, and we’ll confirm your insurance before you arrive.

Can I be seen today if I’m sick?

We keep same-day sick slots open every day and take walk-ins until 6 pm. Video visits are also available within the hour.

Do you take my insurance?

We accept Medicare, Medicaid and most commercial plans. Enter your plan when booking and we’ll check your coverage.

How do I get my lab results?

Results appear in your patient portal as soon as they’re ready, usually within 24 hours, with a note from your doctor.
