Questions

Good to know before your visit

Do you take my insurance?

We’re in-network with most major PPO plans and file claims for you. Send us your details when you book and we’ll confirm your coverage in advance.

Can I get a same-day emergency appointment?

Yes. We hold emergency slots every day. Call before 2 pm and we’ll usually see you the same day.

What payment and financing options do you offer?

We accept all major cards, HSA/FSA and offer 0% financing for 12 months on treatments over $500.

Is there parking nearby?

Street parking on 7th Ave and a garage on 8th St. We’re a 3-minute walk from the F/G at 7th Ave station.
