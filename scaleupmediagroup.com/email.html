The 5 biggest problems

high · Conversion & trust
No call or booking button on the first phone screen
When the page opens on a phone, there's no tap-to-call, 'book' or 'get a quote' button in view; the first one is 5.5 screens down.
What it costs you: Most local searches happen on phones; if the next step isn't visible, visitors bounce.
high · Speed & Core Web Vitals
2 oversized image files (1.5 MB)
2 images are over 300 KB each, 1.5 MB in total, on the homepage alone.
What it costs you: Big images are the most common reason a page loads slowly on a phone.
high · Speed & Core Web Vitals
Main content takes 7.3s to appear on a phone
Largest Contentful Paint measured at 7.3s on a mid-range phone with slow 4G (Google's 'good' is under 2.5s).
What it costs you: Over half of mobile visitors leave a page that takes more than 3 seconds to show anything useful.
high · SEO structure & keywords
No pages for 4 of the 8 areas you mention
The site mentions Brooklyn, Queens, Manhattan, Bronx, Staten Island, Long Island but has no page built to rank in Manhattan, Bronx, Staten Island, Suffolk.
What it costs you: Google ranks pages, not businesses. Without a page for an area, you rarely show up when people there search.
